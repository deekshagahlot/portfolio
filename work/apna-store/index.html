Visual design · Apna Store · iOS · 2025–26

Visual design
showcase

Apna Store set out to build a curation-led shopping experience that stands apart from the noise of mainstream e-commerce. The visual system had to carry that promise on every category page.

The work spanned catalogue photography systems, category landing page design and end-to-end visual direction, shaped in close collaboration with the co-founder, category heads, merchandising and marketing teams.

The core question driving all of it was simple: why should a customer choose Apna Store? Four things had to be true for the answer to land.

  1. Establish a distinct visual identity that reflects Apna Store's curation-first philosophy.
  2. Hold consistent, high quality visual presentation across every product category.
  3. Build trust through quality and intent-led design at every touchpoint.
  4. Let merchandising and category teams communicate product value without visual clutter.

01  ·  Category landing pages

Four categories, one visual language

Each category leads with the argument it wins on, so the header colour, the photography style and the grid rhythm change while the underlying structure stays the same.

Fresh

I used a mix of macro focus and lifestyle setting to highlight the quality of the produce. This was Apna Store’s top selling category.

Groceries

My main objective here was to communicate the largest variety of daily groceries. This was Apna Store’s second highest selling category.

Home & kitchen

This was the first non-food category for Apna Store. The quality-first philosophy carried through here too, through glass surfaces, clean functional type and a palette that extends the one built for the food categories.

Health

The health page needed an even stricter approach to the ingredient list. I wanted it to communicate the purity and quality of these products.

Festive

Here I brought back the colours of the season and communicated the festive mood alongside the usual highly curated products. These examples are from the nine days of Navratri.

Themes

These pages usually drive specific private label products or offers tied to a lifestyle festival, like Valentine’s Day.

03  ·  Colour & type

The system underneath

Category identity is carried almost entirely by one colour and one photographic treatment in the top third of the screen. Everything below it, the grid, the type and the neutrals, is shared.
